Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Vaultix is built with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Storage

All data you enter into Vaultix — including passwords, cards, notes, bank accounts, and documents — is stored exclusively on your device. We do not operate any servers that collect or store your personal data.

Encryption

Your vault database is encrypted using AES-256 via SQLCipher. Your data is protected by your PIN, which is hashed and never stored in plain text.

Google Drive Backup

Vaultix offers an optional Google Drive backup feature. This is entirely opt-in and only triggered manually by you. When used:

  • Your backup file is encrypted before being uploaded
  • Only your own Google Drive storage is used
  • We do not have access to your Google account or Drive files

Google Sign-In

Google Sign-In is used solely to authenticate you with your own Google Drive for backup purposes. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, or any other Google services.

Permissions

  • Camera — Used for card scanning and document scanning only
  • Notifications — Used for reminders you set within the app
  • Internet — Used for password breach checking (HaveIBeenPwned) and optional Google Drive backup

Third-Party Services

Vaultix uses the HaveIBeenPwned API to check if your passwords have been exposed in known data breaches. Only a partial hash of your password is sent — your actual password is never transmitted.

No Ads or Tracking

Vaultix contains no advertisements, no analytics SDKs, and no user tracking of any kind.

Children's Privacy

Vaultix is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
